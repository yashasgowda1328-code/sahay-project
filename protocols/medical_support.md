# Medical Support Protocol

## Objective
Coordinate medical evaluation and care for individuals with HIGH_PRIORITY health indicators.

## Steps
1. Confirm vital signs and medical history.
2. Refer to a qualified medical professional.
3. Ensure transport or telehealth access if needed.
4. Document medical findings in the case file.
5. Coordinate with other services as required.

## Notes
- Do not provide medical advice outside your scope.
- Follow local emergency protocols for acute distress.
