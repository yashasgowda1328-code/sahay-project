# Financial Support Protocol

## Objective
Address immediate financial distress that may impact mental health and safety.

## Steps
1. Assess financial needs and urgency.
2. Provide information on available aid programs.
3. Assist with applications for emergency funds.
4. Coordinate with social services for ongoing support.
5. Document financial assistance provided.

## Notes
- Ensure transparency about eligibility criteria.
- Do not promise funds outside approved programs.
