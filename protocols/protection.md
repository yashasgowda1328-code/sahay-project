# Protection Protocol

## Objective
Ensure immediate safety of individuals at risk of harm.

## Steps
1. Assess the nature and level of risk.
2. Contact relevant protection authorities if required.
3. Provide safe shelter or relocation if necessary.
4. Document all protective measures taken.
5. Coordinate with legal aid if needed.

## Notes
- Prioritize safety over privacy when risk is imminent.
- Maintain confidentiality within legal limits.
