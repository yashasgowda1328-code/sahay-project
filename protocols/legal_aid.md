# Legal Aid Protocol

## Objective
Provide access to legal support for individuals facing legal challenges affecting their wellbeing.

## Steps
1. Identify the legal issue (e.g., custody, housing, employment).
2. Refer to a qualified legal professional.
3. Assist with documentation and case preparation.
4. Follow up on legal proceedings and outcomes.
5. Coordinate with other services as needed.

## Notes
- Do not provide legal advice.
- Maintain client confidentiality within legal limits.
