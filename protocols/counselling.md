# Counselling Protocol

## Objective
Provide immediate emotional support and stabilization for individuals showing HIGH_PRIORITY mental health indicators.

## Steps
1. Establish a safe, private environment.
2. Active listening and validation of feelings.
3. Assess immediate safety and risk of self-harm.
4. Develop a short-term coping plan.
5. Schedule follow-up sessions.

## Notes
- Do not make clinical diagnoses.
- Document all sessions in the case file.
- Escalate to medical support if risk is identified.
