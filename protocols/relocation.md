# Relocation Protocol

## Objective
Facilitate safe relocation for individuals whose safety cannot be assured in their current environment.

## Steps
1. Verify the need for relocation with the case team.
2. Identify safe housing options.
3. Arrange transport and logistics.
4. Provide orientation at the new location.
5. Follow up within 24 hours of relocation.

## Notes
- Coordinate with protection and legal aid teams.
- Document the relocation plan and execution.
