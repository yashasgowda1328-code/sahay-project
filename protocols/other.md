# Other Intervention Protocol

## Objective
Handle interventions that do not fit standard categories.

## Steps
1. Document the nature of the intervention clearly.
2. Identify responsible personnel and timeline.
3. Execute the intervention with appropriate approvals.
4. Record outcomes and lessons learned.
5. Review with the case team for future standardization.

## Notes
- Use this category sparingly.
- Propose new protocol documents for repeated scenarios.
