# Rehabilitation Protocol

## Objective
Support long-term recovery and reintegration for individuals with sustained health deviations.

## Steps
1. Assess rehabilitation needs (physical, psychological, social).
2. Develop a personalized rehabilitation plan.
3. Coordinate with healthcare and social workers.
4. Monitor progress and adjust the plan as needed.
5. Document outcomes and milestones.

## Notes
- Involve the individual in planning their recovery.
- Review the plan regularly with the case team.
